Privacy & Data Handling

Prelaunch disclosure • Draft for owner approval • November 7, 2026

Important prelaunch notice

This page describes known behavior of the current test implementation. Our full privacy policy, verified deletion schedule, and support contact process are not finalized. Until those are confirmed, please do not upload private documents, photographs containing people or addresses, medical or financial records, or other sensitive personal information.

What the tools handle

  • Board Sense: Photographs submitted for board analysis are sent to the Board Sense processing service. The service processes images to produce estimates and may generate annotated blueprint files.
  • Usage limits: The free-analysis allowance is enforced server-side using a privacy-conscious pseudonymous visitor identifier. It is not a guarantee of anonymity.
  • Scrap Radar: Planning inputs such as board weight, buyer category, assumed metal quantities, costs, and case details may be stored in your browser and may be submitted to a configured service when you use a connected feature.
  • Customer account: If you sign in, the account system uses an external authentication provider and may retrieve membership/account status from a connected API. Account registration and recovery may not be enabled yet.
  • Signal Log Preview: Notes entered on the main page are saved only to that browser's local storage. They are not sent to a public guestbook.

Photos and results

Board Sense may produce image previews and blueprint results. Generated blueprint URLs may be accessible to anyone who has the link. Avoid including identifying or sensitive information in any upload. An on-device photo preview may remain in browser storage until removed or expired. Browser data may not transfer between devices or site domains.

Service providers

The test system uses hosting and connected services that may include Vercel, Railway, Supabase, GitHub and external market-data services, depending on the feature. Provider-specific logging and retention have not yet been exhaustively verified for this release.

Retention, deletion, and contact

Not yet verified: Specific server image/blueprint retention periods, backup retention, automatic deletion, service-provider logs, and the owner-approved privacy contact address. Do not assume uploaded files or generated links are automatically deleted after a stated period.

Clearing your browser's storage may remove local entries, but does not necessarily delete files already submitted to a server or copies held by a provider. A formal data-deletion request channel must be confirmed before public launch.

Our launch commitment

We will review this disclosure against deployed services, publish verified retention and contact information, and separate optional accounts and community features from core board-analysis tools. This prelaunch draft is not a completed legal privacy policy.
