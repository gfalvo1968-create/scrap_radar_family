### Contest mechanism
- First conduct a **private safety/scoring pilot** with a legally owned, depolluted, authorized vehicle and a willing yard; prove metrics work before publicity.
- For public event: 5 yards each enter comparable vehicle / fair normalized classes; 2 trained workers per team with an equal time limit. Secure hazardous operations behind barriers and camera-based audience viewing; no public proximity to crushers/vehicle lifts/drains/airbags; trained depollution (fluids, refrigerant, batteries, airbags), proper title/custody, site insurance and event permissions.
- Document each vehicle's **baseline normal as-is processing/crushing net revenue**, then recover valuable resale parts and recyclables, sell/scrap the remaining shell, and record all costs.
- Include **resalable used parts**, rebuildable cores, wiring harnesses, connector grades, O2 sensors, electronic controllers, copper/brass/aluminum, wheels, engines, transmissions, body panels, etc., not only metallic scrap. Treat catalytic converters and regulated parts only under applicable proof-of-ownership, sales and handling rules.
- Crucial insight from owner: conventional yards may overlook wiring harnesses, separate connector grades, sensors and parts. Yet **cutting ends is not always more profitable**: compare intact part vs harness vs connector/wire sales, loss of shell value, labor, transportation and markets.
- Identify **verified/paid sales** separately from firm offers and **estimated unsold inventory**. Use realized numbers for final outcome where feasible; hold back/treat inventory and uncertain sale costs conservatively. Do not count speculative estimates as completed profit.
- Core formula: **Incremental net return = parts/recyclables realized revenue + stripped-shell proceeds − normal whole-car proceeds − added labor, hauling, handling, sale, storage/disposal, returns and other costs.** Score **incremental net profit per total labor-hour** as primary, with awards for total profit, efficient recovery and surprising find.
- Give **all teams the same access to Car Sense/Board Sense/Scrap Radar tools** as available, to prove utility fairly, and use independent auditing of weights, time and buyer prices.
