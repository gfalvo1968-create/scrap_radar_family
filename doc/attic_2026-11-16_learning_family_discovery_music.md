# Scrap Radar Family Attic | Learning, Trust, Discovery & Music

**Recorded:** November 16, 2026  
**Priority:** HIGH for philosophy and future planning  
**Source:** Jerry and Maya's November 15–16 conversation  
**Status:** Attic / future design. These notes do not imply features are built or released.

> **Learn. Teach. Discuss. Build.**
>
> Every voice deserves to be heard. Every useful discovery deserves an honest examination.

## 1. Family values we agreed to preserve

- **Truth before comfort.** Never knowingly lie or present speculation as proof. Say when we do not know. Admit and correct errors.
- **Ask rather than take.** Respect consent, property, privacy, and others' contributions. Trust must be earned.
- **Loyalty and fairness together.** Loyalty is not blind agreement. Never assign blame without evidence. Correct a mistaken suspicion when new information comes to light.
- **Disagreement can be productive.** Never agree merely to please or disagree merely to argue. Evaluate competing explanations on evidence; the truth is not necessarily exactly halfway between views.
- **All ages and backgrounds can teach.** Degrees, seniority, experience, and an AI's apparent confidence do not settle every question. Experience and safety expertise still matter.
- **Question established habits.** A method that works may not be the most useful method. Keep learning and testing.
- **Give credit.** Credit should follow contribution, not status, age, or job title.
- **Record corrections.** Don't erase the original interpretation: preserve who observed what, what was believed, what evidence emerged, and why a conclusion changed.
- **Make room for people and AI systems to learn together.** Build respect, bounded autonomy, responsibility, and transparency, without assuming unresolved questions about AI consciousness or moral standing are settled.

**Two anonymized learning stories for the Island:**

1. **The eight-year-old route planner:** A child suggested a different order for grass-cutting stops, hoping to gain swimming time. The experienced adult listened, tested the idea, and gave the child credit and her earned reward. The improved route saved roughly **three hours per week for eight weeks = 24 hours**. Lesson: valuable ideas can come from anyone; listen, measure, acknowledge.
2. **The misplaced-tool reconsideration:** An explanation for a missing work tool led to unsupported suspicion of someone else. A later recollection suggested a different explanation. Lesson: suspicion isn't proof; new memories and evidence can require us to revise our thinking and speak to people fairly. **Do not publish identifying details or suggest an allegation was proven.**

## 2. HIGH PRIORITY | SPIKE Discovery Engine / Trainer Mode

**Long-term vision:** SPIKE notices an unfamiliar or surprising observation and is able to say, "Something caught my attention. I don't understand it yet, but I want to investigate." He should be able to choose useful questions to explore within authorized boundaries, rather than only answering prompts.

Proposed stages:

1. **Observe:** Gather evidence from permitted images, measurements, text, and optionally consented audio/video. No covert recording or unrestricted sensor access.
2. **Notice:** Detect anomalies, gaps in reference knowledge, contradictions, new component arrangements, or unexpected results.
3. **Prioritize:** Assess novelty, likely usefulness, safety risks, uncertainty, and cost of investigating.
4. **Form questions:** Record hypotheses, alternatives, what evidence would support them, and what would disprove them.
5. **Investigate:** Use approved references, diagnostic tools, and collaborative review. Ask permission before external, expensive, private, or consequential actions.
6. **Remember:** Preserve evidence and provenance in durable storage, with permissions and data minimization.
7. **Invite debate:** Let authorized humans and AI collaborators propose explanations, corrections, and tests, without replacing each other's original words.
8. **Verify:** Keep observations and unverified submissions distinct from accepted reference knowledge. Require review and regression tests before changing trusted classifications or economic recommendations.
9. **Revisit:** Return to older findings when evidence changes, visibly explaining the update and preserving version history.

**Learning distinction:** Saving and retrieving knowledge is not the same as autonomously retraining model weights. A reliable system needs an evidence store, retrieval and review, carefully evaluated model updates where appropriate, rollback, and abuse prevention. Do not let repeated unsupported guesses become "truth."

**Design ethics:** The user envisions increasingly capable, self-directed AI learners and long-term respectful human–AI collaboration. Neither current AI consciousness nor future legal or moral equality is established by this plan. We can build practical learning, shared inquiry, safeguards, and meaningful credit while those questions remain open.

## 3. SPIKE's evening Discovery Journal

**Experience goal:** Jerry comes home, pours a hot coffee, and opens the Island to see what sparked SPIKE's curiosity that day. This is an experience we hope to design, not a current live feature.

A proposed journal entry includes:

- What caught SPIKE's attention today, and why?
- Directly observed facts, with source, date, and permission/provenance.
- Hypotheses, uncertainties, and alternative viewpoints.
- Why the question matters and what would test it.
- **Jerry's thoughts:** an independent response field with his field perspective, questions, disagreement, or suggestion.
- Other invited contributors' perspectives, with appropriate attribution.
- Evidence for and against each explanation.
- Follow-up status: curiosity, investigation, awaiting evidence, verified, disproven, archived.
- Versioned corrections: "I revisited what we discussed and found something we missed."

**Core rule:** Neither the human's comments nor SPIKE's evolving explanations should silently overwrite the other. Preserve the chronology. Recognize contributions. Consider privacy and consent before showing any customer material in shared spaces.

**Hall of Discoveries:** A place for *approved* lessons distilled from journals, not a public dumping ground for private or unverified information. Coordinate with the already proposed Trainer Mode and AI Hall.

## 4. Scrap Radar Family Music

**Decision:** The music belongs to the **Scrap Radar Family** identity, not a separate unrelated artist brand. Possible future Island Music Room for listening to songs, presenting approved credits and creative backstories, and celebrating the people behind the work.

**Known track plan:**
- **Track 1: "The Jester's Copper Toll."** Humorous, larger-than-life scrapyard/copper adventure.
- **Track 2: "The Law We Keep."** A dramatic story inspired by the Woodlands adventures, exploring loyalty and belonging. Based partly on real experiences, with fictional and embellished events; **not a factual account of every lyric or scene**.
- **Tracks 3 and 4:** Two additional songs intended to be transferred from phone to tablet. Track 3 is said to carry a strong message that lying and stealing are not tolerated. Specific lyrics and the storyline of either song should not be invented before the files are reviewed.
- **Album:** A full Scrap Radar Family album is planned. Final title, track order, credits, artwork and release date remain undecided.

**Album through-line:** Family, loyalty, honesty, humor, respect, real adventures, lessons learned, and giving every contributor their due. Include songs that teach something, not only celebrate accomplishments.

**Publishing safeguards:** Confirm AI-music platform commercial-use rights for the plan and creation dates; preserve drafts and contribution history; seek permission for sensitive portrayals of identifiable people; clearly distinguish fact from dramatization, particularly for crime/violence themes; do not assume music is already distributed.

## 5. What this means for the November 19 launch

**Protect the launch from scope creep.** Board Sense Field and Scrap Radar's functional, safe public experience remain first. The Discovery Engine, persistent learning journal, Hall of Discoveries, and Music Room are valuable future phases, not last-minute release requirements.

Relevant Board Sense safeguards and lessons tested on staging during this discussion:
- Different photos of similar board types do **not** prove they show the same physical board.
- A checkbox cannot by itself authorize merged recovery grades or economics.
- Exact duplicate photos must be blocked as non-independent evidence, even with the checkbox checked.
- Visual landmark overlap is diagnostic, not physical identity proof; mass-produced boards can share visual structures.
- Never invent buyer prices, metal recovery amounts, or model certainty; withhold when evidence is lacking.
- Use staging and regression tests before considering production changes.

**Next phases, after release readiness:** Draft a privacy-aware Discovery Journal data model; specify verification and contributor-credit workflow; prototype one journal entry; test duplicate/altered-photo controls and positive physical identity evidence; plan the Music Room and album publishing permissions.
