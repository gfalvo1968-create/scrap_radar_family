Service Information & Limitations

Prelaunch disclosure • Draft for owner approval • November 7, 2026

Before you rely on a result

Scrap Radar Family's public tools are in development. This document is a plain-language description of their limitations, not finalized customer terms or a purchase agreement.

Board Sense

Board and component recognition may be wrong. Identity confidence, visual grades, and observed-feature scores are heuristic model assessments, not independent inspections, assays, or confirmed buyer classifications. Never infer recovered precious-metal weight or plating composition from the image alone.

Scrap Radar and prices

Market references, historical buyer samples, and user-entered recovery scenarios are planning aids, not binding purchase offers, guarantees, or investment forecasts. Buyers have their own acceptance and preparation requirements. Obtain an actual quote and weigh the material before making a sale decision.

Physical work and safety

Recycling, dismantling, electronics repair, and metal recovery involve possible electrical, fire, chemical, and physical hazards. Do not use AI instructions alone as authority for hazardous processing or for opening equipment that may contain charged capacitors.

Accounts, payments, and future features

Future houses, stores, schools, equipment rescue programs, and community jobs are proposals, not services currently offered. Do not assume purchases, employment, shop accounts, or payment processing are available until announced and verified.

Usage and availability

Public sample access may be limited. A service outage may temporarily prevent an analysis. An analysis performed by the system does not certify the board's origin or legal ownership.

Required before public launch

The owner must approve final usage terms, business identity and contact details, payment/refund terms if applicable, copyright/user-upload permissions, and applicable legal requirements. Nothing on this draft page authorizes a paid transaction.
